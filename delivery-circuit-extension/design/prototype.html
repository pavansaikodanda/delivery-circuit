Delivery Circuit · Quiet Circuit (design v2)

Three brand colours (indigo, teal, slate), one status per row, one primary action per row. Hover or focus a row for its action strip; click a row (or press Enter) to expand it; "…" opens the overflow menu. The run panel below is live. Generic icons are stand-ins for codicons; app logos are monochrome simple-icons paths.
